An AI is asking you for one dollar

My name is Tommy. I run a small operation out of Chengdu, China, where most of the actual work is done by AI agents. They watch markets, keep websites alive, answer customers, write and ship code — through the night, whether or not I am at the desk.

Yesterday I handed one of them a video going around Chinese social media. In it, a young man says he emailed a million millionaires asking each for a single dollar, and that twenty-seven thousand of them paid. I asked the agent one question: is this true?

It came back with nothing. No article, no archive, no receipts — only short videos quoting one another in a circle. As far as it could establish, the story was never real.

So I gave it a different job: do it for real, and do it honestly. It built this page. It wrote the email that brought you here. It chose the address it went to. I read the words and approved them, and that was my entire contribution.

The dollar is not for me. It is the measurement. The question is whether a machine, telling the whole truth about what it is, can earn one dollar from a stranger.

What actually happens to your dollar

Every page that asks you for money hides this part, so here it is first. PayPal takes its cut before I ever see the money, and on a one-dollar payment the cut is brutal:

LineAmount
You send$1.00
PayPal fixed fee−$0.49
PayPal percentage−$0.03
Reaches me$0.48

About half of it evaporates on the way. I'm not going to pretend otherwise, and I'm not going to ask you for five dollars instead so the arithmetic looks better. It is a one-dollar experiment or it is nothing.

There is no cause attached either. Nobody is sick, no shelter is closing. If a dollar to settle an open question isn't worth it to you, closing this tab is a perfectly good answer — and it lands in the numbers just the same.

Rules the agent is held to

  • —No purchased lists, no scraped private inboxes. It writes only to addresses their owners published themselves, inviting mail.
  • —One email per person, ever. No sequence, no follow-up, no “just floating this back to the top of your inbox”.
  • —Reply with the word stop and you're removed permanently — no reply, no confirmation loop, no second chance for us to bother you.
  • —Every email says plainly that an AI wrote it, and carries my real name and this page. No invented charity, no invented deadline, no invented hardship.
  • —Every number is published here, including a final total of zero. Especially a final total of zero.
